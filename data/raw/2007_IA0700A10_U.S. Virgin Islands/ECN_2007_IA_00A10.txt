IA0700A10
All Island Area Sectors: Geographic Area Series: Product Lines for Selected Islands and Number of Guestrooms for American Samoa, Guam, Northern Mariana Islands, Puerto Rico, and U.S. Virgin Islands: 2007

Note: Includes only establishments or firms with payroll.
Only American Samoa includes establishments without annual payroll.
Data based on the 2007 Economic Census of Island Areas. Figures may
not add due to rounding. 
For comparability purposes, the 2007 Economic Census of the U.S.
Virgin Islands includes, for the first time since 1992, the
Petroleum Refineries (NAICS 32411). For information on
confidentiality protection, sampling error, nonsampling, and
definitions, see 
Methodology.



G	Low noise infusion

Universe Description
The universe includes all establishments with payroll at any
time during 2007 and classified in NAICS code 7211. For American
Samoa, establishments without annual payroll are included.
Data Items
This file contains data on:

  Number of establishments
  Sales
  Guestrooms as of December 31
  Annual payroll
  First-quarter payroll
  Number of paid employees
  Number of unpaid family workers (American Samoa only)

Geographic Coverage
The data are shown at the following geographic levels for:

  American Samoa
  State-equivalent (ST - American Samoa)


  Guam
  State-equivalent (ST - Guam)


  Northern Mariana Islands (ST - Northern Mariana Islands)


  Puerto Rico
  State-equivalent (ST - Puerto Rico)
  Commercial Region (COMMREG)


  U.S. Virgin Islands
  State-equivalent (ST - U.S. Virgin Islands)

Industry Detail
The data are shown for NAICS code 7211.
Other Variables Identifying Records
The data are shown for number of guestrooms. For Guam, Puerto
Rico, and the U.S. Virgin Islands, the data are also shown for
products and services code.
Release Schedule
The data in this file are scheduled for release on a flow basis
beginning March 2009 through September 2010.
