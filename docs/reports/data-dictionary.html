Primary sources

A. USVIBER Annual Tourism Indicators

Field Value
Files TOUR20-revised.pdf, TOUR22.pdf, TOUR24.pdf
Source U.S. Virgin Islands Bureau of Economic Research
URL usviber.org
Coverage 2000, 2010–2024 (TOUR24); earlier reports extend backward
Frequency Annual
Geography USVI territory; some variables by island (St. Thomas/St. John, St. Croix)

Key variables:

Variable Units Definition
total_visitors Thousands All visitors: tourists + excursionists
tourists Thousands Overnight visitors (stay ≥ 1 night)
excursionists Thousands Same-day visitors (cruise + air day-trip)
air_excursionists Thousands Same-day visitors arriving by air
cruise_passengers Thousands Cruise ship day visitors
air_visitors Thousands All arrivals by air (tourists by air + air excursionists) — not all tourists
cruise_ships Count Cruise ship calls
expenditures_total $M, nominal Total visitor spending in-territory
expenditures_tourists $M, nominal Overnight-visitor spending only
expenditures_excursionists $M, nominal Same-day visitor spending only
total_rooms Count Hotel rooms + condo/vacation rental units
hotel_rooms Count Hotel rooms only
hotel_count Count Number of operating hotels
occupancy_rate_pct Percent Territory-wide average hotel occupancy
emp_tourism_total Jobs Tourism-related employment (DOL survey; ends 2022)

Data quality flags:

  • air_excursionists (2020): value of 216.7k is identical to year 2000 — physically implausible for a COVID year. Cross-check with TOUR20-revised.pdf pending.
  • excursionists (2000, 2010): does not equal air_excursionists + cruise_passengers within rounding (discrepancies of −14.9k and +149.7k respectively). Likely definitional residuals or data revisions between report editions.
  • air_visitors is not equal to tourists + air_excursionists because not all tourists arrive by air. Do not use as an arrival-mode identity.

B. FRED Leisure & Hospitality Employment

Field Value
Files fred_SMS78000007000000001.csv (SA), fred_SMU78000007000000001.csv (NSA)
Source U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics via FRED
Coverage January 1990 – September 2026
Frequency Monthly
Units Thousands of persons (e.g., 8.0 = 8,000 workers)
Scope NAICS 71 + 72 — Arts, entertainment, recreation, accommodation, and food services

Aggregation rule: Use the mean of monthly SA observations for annual comparisons. Do not sum monthly values to compute annual employment.

Limitation: Leisure & Hospitality is broader than tourism-specific employment. It includes museums, performing arts, casinos (NAICS 71) alongside hotels and restaurants (NAICS 72).

C. NPS Recreation Visitation

Field Value
Files VIIS_*.csv, BUIS_*.csv (annual and monthly)
Source NPS IRMA Public Use Statistics
URL irma.nps.gov/Stats
Parks Virgin Islands NP (VIIS, St. John); Buck Island Reef NM (BUIS, St. Croix)
Coverage VIIS: 1957–2025 annual; BUIS: 1962–2025 annual (gap 1999–2001)
Frequency Annual and monthly
Units Recreation visits (individual visits, not unique visitors)

Important definitions: - NPS recreation visits ≠ territory-wide tourist arrivals - A person may visit multiple times; the park counts each visit separately - Park closures and access conditions affect counts (October–December 2017: near-zero counts due to hurricane damage and closure) - Cruise passengers rarely enter either park; visitation is dominated by independent overnight visitors

2026 exclusion: 2026 monthly VIIS data shows 100,000–170,000 visits per month (January–June), approximately three times the 2025 equivalent months. This anomaly has not been explained; 2026 data is excluded from all analysis.

Aggregation rule: Sum monthly observations for annual totals; do not use the annual-file total directly if computing from monthly data (sums match exactly for all available years).

D. World Bank WDI

Field Value
Files VIR.ST.INT.ARVL.json, VIR.ST.INT.RCPT.CD.json
Indicators International tourism arrivals (ST.INT.ARVL); receipts in current USD (ST.INT.RCPT.CD)
Coverage 1995–2020 (non-null values)
Frequency Annual

Arrivals: 2020 value of 8,612,000 is physically impossible (COVID year; normal range ~1.7–2.8M). Set to NaN. Use USVIBER as primary arrivals source.

Receipts vs. USVIBER expenditures: World Bank receipts run approximately 70–80% of USVIBER total expenditures in overlapping years. This is a definitional difference, not a data error. World Bank receipts likely exclude some in-territory spending categories that USVIBER captures. Do not use interchangeably.

E. U.S. Census Bureau — Economic Census

Vintage Files Census years Key table
2007 ECN_2007_IA_00A01_with_ann.csv 2002 + 2007 A01 (2-digit)
2007 ECN_2007_IA_00A10_with_ann.csv 2007 A10 (rooms, NAICS 7211)
2012 IA1200A02.dat 2012 A02 (2–5 digit)
2012 IA1200A10.dat 2012 A10 (rooms, NAICS 7211)
2017 IA1700BASIC02.dat 2012 + 2017 BASIC02
2017 IA1700NAPCS02.dat 2017 NAPCS02 (product breakdown)
2022 IA2200BASIC02.dat 2017 + 2022 BASIC02
2022 IA2200NAPCS02.dat 2022 NAPCS02

Geographic filter: Filter to GEOTYPE = 2 (territory level) for all .dat files. Island- and town-level rows (GEOTYPE 3, 5) will otherwise inflate territory totals.

Employment reference date: March pay period of the census year.

Guestrooms (RMRTRAN): Reference date is December 31 of the census year.

  • 2007 and 2012: use A10 industry-total row (PSCODE/NAPCS = industry total)
  • 2017 and 2022: use NAPCS product code 7003825000 (“Room or unit accommodation for travelers”) — the NAPCS Total row RMRTRAN is inconsistent across vintages and should not be used for guestroom counts.

Critical note on 2017: The Economic Census guestroom reference date is December 31, 2017 — approximately three months after Hurricanes Irma and Maria (September 6 and 19). The 2017 guestroom count (2,624) reflects post-hurricane inventory, not the pre-hurricane baseline. It should be labeled accordingly and not used as a pre-storm benchmark.

Monetary units: All Census monetary variables (RCPTOT, PAYANN) are in thousands of dollars ($1,000). Divide by 1,000 for millions.

Suppression flags: Cells with flag D are withheld to avoid disclosing individual company data. S = withheld for reliability. Do not treat suppressed cells as zero.

F. BEA — U.S. Virgin Islands GDP

Field Value
File vigdp0624.xlsx
Source U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis
URL bea.gov
Coverage GDP (Table 1.1): 2002–2022; Industry value added (Tables 2.1–2.3): 2002–2021
Frequency Annual
Units Millions of dollars (nominal) or millions of chained (2012) dollars (real)

Note: BEA discontinued regular USVI territory GDP updates after 2022 when Office of Insular Affairs funding ended.

Table guide:

Table Content Coverage
1.1 Nominal GDP by expenditure component 2002–2022
1.2 Real GDP (chained 2012 $) 2002–2022
2.1 Nominal value added by industry 2002–2021
2.3 Real value added by industry (chained) 2002–2021

Important: “Accommodation and food services” value added is not equivalent to tourism GDP. It excludes tourism-related retail, transportation, and recreation, and includes consumption by residents.

Do not deflate BEA real series — they are already expressed in constant (2012) dollars using BEA’s own price indexes.

G. USVI CPI

Field Value
File Econ21.pdf (USVIBER Annual Economic Indicators 2021)
Coverage 2009–2021, annual
Base year 2009 = 100

Known issue: The 2018 value (99.8) represents a sharp drop from 2017 (130.0), which is implausible as a true price deflation. The 2017 hurricanes may have caused statistical complications, or there may be a column alignment error in the PDF. Do not use for deflation across the 2017–2018 boundary without verifying directly with USVIBER.

Coverage gap: No reliable USVI CPI series is available for 2022–2025. All nominal monetary series in this analysis are presented without deflation for years after 2021.
